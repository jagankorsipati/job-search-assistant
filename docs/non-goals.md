# V1 Non-Goals

## Phase 6 release status, 2026-09-14

Phase 6 does not add automatic drafting, semantic verification, broad DOCX support, batch proposal combination, retained generated files, or submission. Exact paragraph resolution is not fact extraction. Rendering and release gates remain open; see [Phase 6 evidence](security/phase-6-verification.md).

V1 will not:

- Submit applications or accept terms on a user's behalf.
- Log in to LinkedIn or another job board.
- Bypass CAPTCHAs, bot controls, rate limits, or access restrictions.
- Promise comprehensive or real-time job discovery.
- Invent or infer unsupported career claims.
- Rank candidates using protected characteristics.
- make hiring, immigration, compensation, or legal decisions.
- Send messages, emails, or follow-ups automatically.
- Publish résumés or profiles publicly.
- Expose the service through router port forwarding.
- Build microservices, Kubernetes infrastructure, or an event platform.
- Train models on household data.
- Share one household member's documents with another by default.

These boundaries may be reconsidered only through documented decisions and threat review.
