# Product Vision

## Problem

Job seekers repeatedly copy job descriptions, compare requirements, adjust documents, and track applications across disconnected tools. Generic automation can save time but can also expose personal data, create inaccurate résumés, or submit poor applications without review.

## Vision

Provide each household member with a private job-search workspace that reduces administrative work while keeping the person in control of every career claim and application decision.

## Target users

- A household member actively applying for jobs
- A trusted household administrator maintaining the installation, without automatic access to another member's career content

## Outcomes

- Relevant opportunities are easier to organize.
- Job fit and gaps are visible and explainable.
- Tailored materials remain accurate and reviewable.
- Application status and follow-ups are not lost.
- Sensitive career data remains under household control.

## Principles

1. Truth before optimization.
2. User approval before export or external action.
3. Private by default and isolated by user.
4. Reliable manual ingestion before fragile automation.
5. Deterministic rules around optional AI.
6. A simple modular monolith before distributed services.
